sdr-web: a software-defined radio in your browser

Every block of a radio link, simulated in plain JavaScript with no install, no server and no libraries. Modulate a message, push it through a channel with noise, fading, multipath and hardware impairments, then watch a receiver recover it stage by stage: matched filtering, frequency, timing and carrier recovery, equalization and decisions. Every number can be checked against theory, and every run is reproducible from its seed.

AM

Envelope vs coherent detection, over-modulation and the threshold effect.

FM

Bessel sidebands, Carson's rule, clicks, emphasis, and hearing a fading channel.

QPSK

A full digital link: RRC, CFO, Gardner timing, PLL, equalizers, fading and the receiver at every stage.

BER

Monte Carlo sweeps with confidence intervals against theory, implementation loss, and analog SNR curves.
