sdr-web

A software-defined radio simulator that runs entirely in your browser: AM, FM and QPSK modulators and demodulators, a channel model with noise and impairments, and Monte Carlo bit-error-rate curves. No install, no server, no libraries. Just open this file.

  • AM: amplitude modulation (coming soon)
  • FM: frequency modulation (coming soon)
  • QPSK: digital modem with constellation view (coming soon)
  • BER: Monte Carlo BER vs. theory (coming soon)
  • Tests: unit tests and plot-library demos for the core DSP code

Phase 0: foundations (RNG, complex math, FFT, DSP, plotting, tests).
