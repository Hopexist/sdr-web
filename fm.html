FM: frequency modulation

What am I looking at?

FM basics. Frequency modulation keeps the amplitude constant and lets the message move the frequency: the instantaneous frequency is Δf·m(t), so with |m| ≤ 1 the carrier swings by at most ±Δf (the peak deviation). At complex baseband the signal is e^{jφ(t)} with φ the running integral of 2πΔf·m. That's why the clean I/Q scatter is a ring of radius 1: only the angle carries information. The simulation runs the RF part at 384 kHz (8× the 48 kHz audio rate) so broadcast FM fits.

β and Carson's rule. The modulation index β = Δf/W compares the deviation with the message bandwidth W. A tone produces spectral lines at multiples of its frequency with powers Jn(β)² (turn on "Show Bessel"), and at β ≈ 2.405 the carrier line vanishes entirely. Carson's rule BT ≈ 2(Δf + W) captures about 98% of the power. The "occupied BW (98%)" readout measures it directly.

Trading bandwidth for SNR. Above threshold the output SNR is 3β²(β+1)·CNR for a tone (CNR measured in BT), or 1.5β² times the baseband SNR γ. Doubling the deviation buys ~6 dB, paid for with bandwidth. AM can never beat γ. In the SNR curve, each FM line sits above the AM reference by 10·log10(1.5β²) once it is above threshold.

Threshold and clicks. When noise inside BT becomes comparable to the carrier (CNR around 10 dB), the noise phasor occasionally swings around the origin. The phase jumps by 2π and the discriminator outputs a sharp spike: a click. Click rate rises steeply as CNR falls, and below threshold the output SNR collapses far faster than the CNR. Watch the instantaneous-frequency plot and the click counter as you lower the CNR.

Pre- and de-emphasis. FM detector noise grows with frequency (its spectrum rises as f²), so most of it lands at the top of the audio band, where speech and music have little energy. Pre-emphasis boosts the high frequencies before modulation, and the receiver's de-emphasis cuts them back, taking that noise with it. That's typically a 10–13 dB gain for broadcast FM. A carrier frequency offset just shifts the discriminator output by a constant, which the DC blocker removes.
